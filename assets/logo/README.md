# Spiti / Porch

The roofline is the face and a small doorway cuts into its base. Two open eyes mean ready, narrowed eyes accompany focus, and raised arcs acknowledge completion. The orange porch-light graphic can expand across a poster while the silhouette stays fixed.

[Identity board](identity-board.jpg) · [Interactive companion](companion.html) · [Home specimen](home-example.png)

## Visual language

Use the standalone [symbol](icon.svg) below 80 px of available width. Give the [signature](lockup.svg) clear space equal to one quarter of the symbol height. The [wordmark](wordmark.svg) and signature have outlined type and the three vectors inherit colour when placed inline.

Set headlines in Bricolage Grotesque and body copy in Inter. The primary palette uses ink #34231E, paper #FFF4DD, accent #F76C42 and secondary #F9C572. Use ink for small text on light backgrounds. Keep the eyes open and clear in the favicon. Type files, licences and implementation colours are in [fonts](fonts/) and [tokens.css](tokens.css).

## Face and interface

One resident represents the Mac. A change of expression accompanies the visible status. Introduce a working state when an activity starts, and show completion only after it finishes. The [expression sheet](expressions.svg) shows the master in three states. Blinking and pointer gaze stop under reduced motion. The interactive study uses fictional local data.

Fabricator UI informed the stable body and expressive eyes. Grok Bot informed the use of recurring identity and visible activity in the workspace. [BotFace documentation](https://fabricator-ui.vercel.app/docs/components/base/bot-face) · [Grok Bot design notes](https://x.ai/news/designing-grok-bot)
